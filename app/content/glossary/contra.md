In a contra, the sale of the home you own and the purchase of the next one are completed together. The cash from the sale (after your loan and the CPF refund) goes straight into the purchase, so you do not need that cash up front.

It only works when both dates line up. An HDB resale completes within about {policy:sellbuy.resale.completion_days} days of HDB accepting the resale application, so the two transactions are timed together.

If the dates cannot line up, the usual ways around it are to stay on in the sold flat for a while (extension of stay) or to borrow the gap for a short time (bridging loan).

Tip: Ask HDB and your lawyer early whether your sale and purchase can be completed together. The Plan tab shows the cash and CPF that come back from the sale.
