Often called the Family Grant, this grant helps first-timer families buy a flat on the resale market. Unlike the EHG it is not tiered by income: eligible households receive a fixed amount set by flat size — a higher amount for two- to four-room flats, a lower one for five-room and larger flats — provided household income does not exceed {policy:eligibility.income_ceiling.family} a month.

The grant is credited to the buyers' CPF accounts and used towards the purchase. At least one buyer must be a Singapore Citizen. Lower amounts apply when the other buyer is a Permanent Resident, when only one buyer is a first-timer, and to singles.

Worked example: e.g. a first-timer couple buying a four-room resale flat could combine this grant with the EHG (if their income qualifies) and the Proximity Housing Grant, reducing the loan or downpayment they need.

Tip: Because grants are paid into CPF, they count as CPF used for the flat and are refunded to CPF with accrued interest when it is sold.
