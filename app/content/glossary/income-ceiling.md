HDB uses household income to target its help. For families, both the HDB concessionary loan and the CPF Housing Grant for resale flats require an average gross monthly household income of no more than {policy:eligibility.income_ceiling.family}; a single buying alone faces a ceiling of {policy:eligibility.income_ceiling.single}. The Enhanced CPF Housing Grant has its own, lower ceiling and is tiered by income below it.

Income is usually averaged over the twelve months before the application and covers the gross income of all buyers and, in some cases, other listed occupiers.

Exceeding a ceiling does not stop a household from buying a resale flat; it means borrowing from a bank instead of HDB, or missing out on a grant.

Worked example: e.g. a household earning `S$15,000` a month is compared against each ceiling separately — it can be above one and below another.
