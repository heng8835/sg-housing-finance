At {policy:cpf.age.ra_formation}, CPF moves savings into a new **Retirement Account**: first from the Special Account, then from the Ordinary Account, until the **Full Retirement Sum (FRS)** for your year is reached. There are three levels — the **Basic Retirement Sum (BRS)**, the FRS, and the **Enhanced Retirement Sum (ERS)**, {policy:cpf.retirement_sums.ers_multiple} times the FRS, the most you can put in.

If you own a property with enough lease left, you can **pledge** it and set aside only the BRS in cash; the rest above it may be withdrawn, but the CPF LIFE payout is then lower.

Tip: CPF used for a home is not in the RA. Plan → CPF & retirement shows the RA at {policy:cpf.age.ra_formation} with and without the flat you picked, and ticks each sum met.
