The rules for Permanent Residents differ from those for citizens in four ways that matter for a home:

- **New flats**: a household of Permanent Residents with no citizen cannot buy a new flat from HDB.
- **Resale flats**: a PR household can buy a resale flat once every PR in it has held PR for the minimum period HDB sets. Non-Malaysian PR households must also fit the block and neighbourhood **SPR quota**.
- **Loans and grants**: an HDB loan needs at least one Singapore Citizen buyer, so PR-only households borrow from a bank. CPF housing grants also need a citizen buyer.
- **Stamp duty**: Permanent Residents pay Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty, from their first home.

A **single** PR cannot buy an HDB flat at all; renting, a resale EC or a private home are the routes until citizenship.

Tip: Set each buyer's residency in Your household — the app then shows only the paths and loans that apply.
