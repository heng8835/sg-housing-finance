If you buy the next home before the sale of your current one completes, the downpayment is due before the sale money arrives. A bridging loan from a bank covers that gap for a short time and is repaid from the sale proceeds.

Bridging loans come from banks, not HDB. They charge interest for the time you use them, and the bank looks at your income and at the sale you have already agreed.

Tip: Compare the cost of a bridging loan with the other ways to bridge the gap — a contra (both completions together) or an extension of stay — before you commit. This app does not add a bridging loan to its numbers.
