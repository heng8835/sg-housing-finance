When a whole HDB flat is rented out, HDB counts the tenants who are not Singapore Citizens or Malaysians. Each block and each neighbourhood has a cap on the share of flats rented to them; once a block reaches its cap, an owner there cannot rent the whole flat to another such tenant until the count falls.

The quota is about the **landlord's block**, not about you. A Permanent Resident or a foreigner looking for a whole flat should ask the landlord whether the block is under the quota before paying a deposit — HDB has an online check by block. Renting a **room** is not limited by this quota.

Tenancies for non-citizens are also approved for a limited period at a time, so a long stay means renewing with HDB.

Tip: Rent & Buy → "What your household can do" lists the renting rules that apply to your household.
