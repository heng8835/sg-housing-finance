HDB classifies buyers by their housing history. A first-timer has not previously enjoyed a housing subsidy — for example, has not bought a new flat from HDB or a resale flat with a CPF housing grant. Owning private property also affects eligibility for grants.

A couple are first-timers when both partners are. If one has already received a subsidy, the household is treated as "first-timer and second-timer", which has its own grant amounts.

This matters for affordability. First-timer families can receive the CPF Housing Grant and the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant on a resale flat, while second-timer households are mainly limited to the Proximity Housing Grant.

Worked example: e.g. a couple where one partner earlier bought a new flat from HDB with their parents would generally not count as a full first-timer household.

Tip: The HFE letter confirms each buyer's status, which removes the guesswork.
