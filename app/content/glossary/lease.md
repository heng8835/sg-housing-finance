Buying an HDB flat means buying the right to live in it for what is left of a {policy:lease.term.years}-year lease, not owning the land outright. The lease starts from the block's lease commencement date, not from when the current owner bought it.

A resale buyer takes over whatever is left of the lease. When the lease runs out, the flat returns to HDB and the owner receives nothing for it. This is why lease length matters for value, for financing and for CPF rules.

Worked example: e.g. a flat whose lease began `40` years ago has {policy:lease.term.years} minus `40` years left.

Tip: The lease commencement date is published with HDB's resale transaction data; it is not the same as the year the flat was last sold.
