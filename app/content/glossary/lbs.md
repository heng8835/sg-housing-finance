With the Lease Buyback Scheme you keep a shorter lease — chosen by the age of the youngest owner, up to {policy:seniors.lbs.retain_max} years — and HDB buys the rest of the lease from you. You stay in the same flat; nothing changes day to day.

The money goes first into each owner's **Retirement Account (RA)**, up to the amount HDB requires, and from there into **CPF LIFE**, which pays out monthly for life. After the required top-ups, up to {policy:seniors.lbs.cash_retention} of what is left (all owners together) is paid in cash; above that, each owner is first topped up towards the Full Retirement Sum and the rest is paid in cash too. Household income must be within {policy:seniors.lbs.income_ceiling} a month.

Tip: the app does not estimate LBS proceeds — HDB works them out from a valuation of your flat. The **Options** card in Plan shows whether the scheme may be open to you; HDB's own estimator gives the figures.
