When you sell first and your next home is not ready yet, you can ask for a temporary extension of stay: you stay on in the flat you sold for up to {policy:sellbuy.extension_of_stay.max_months} months after the resale completes, with no further extension.

The buyer must agree, and it is only for sellers who have already committed to buy a completed home (an exercised Option to Purchase or a signed sale agreement). You receive the sale money on completion, so the next purchase can use it, and you avoid moving twice.

Tip: Agree the length and terms with the buyer early, before you grant the Option to Purchase.
