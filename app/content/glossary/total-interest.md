term: Total interest cost
short: The sum of all interest paid over the life of a loan — all instalments added up, minus the amount originally borrowed.
formula: "Total interest = instalment × number of months − loan amount"

Total interest is the price of borrowing. It depends on the loan size, the rate and, very strongly, the tenure: stretching the loan over more years lowers the instalment but means paying interest for longer.

Worked example: e.g. a `S$400,000` loan at an illustrative `3.2%`. Over `20` years the instalment is about `S$2,260` and total interest about `S$142,000`; over `30` years the instalment drops to about `S$1,730`, but total interest rises to about `S$223,000`.

Total interest on the loan is separate from the cost of using CPF. CPF savings used for the flat also build up accrued interest, which must be refunded to the owners' CPF accounts when the flat is sold.

Tip: Looking at total interest alongside the monthly instalment shows the full trade-off between tenures, not just the monthly one.
