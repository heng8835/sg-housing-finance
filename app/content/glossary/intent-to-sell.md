Before selling, you register an Intent to Sell on the HDB Flat Portal, and HDB checks that you are eligible to sell — for example, that the Minimum Occupation Period is met.

The Intent to Sell must be valid and registered at least {policy:sellbuy.intent_to_sell.days_before_otp} days before you grant an Option to Purchase to a buyer. Use that time to get your CPF statement (CPF used and accrued interest) and to plan where you will live next.

Tip: If you plan to sell and buy, register early so the sale and the next purchase can be timed together.
