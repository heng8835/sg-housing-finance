term: Service & conservancy charges (S&CC)
short: S&CC is a monthly fee your town council charges for cleaning, lifts and upkeep of common areas — it is separate from grants and ABSD, which are about buying. Town councils set different rates by household type: the lower rate is for citizen households without private, industrial or commercial property. We use the lower rate when a buyer is a Singapore Citizen, so check your town council for the exact rate.

Every HDB flat owner pays service and conservancy charges to the Town Council that manages the estate. The money funds cleaning, lift maintenance, landscaping, common-area lighting, and a sinking fund for longer-term works such as repainting and re-roofing.

Charges are set by each Town Council and differ by flat type — larger flats pay more — and by town. Eligible Singaporean households receive periodic S&CC rebates from the Government, credited directly to their Town Council accounts.

Worked example: e.g. if S&CC were `S$80` a month, that is `S$960` a year — a small but permanent addition to housing costs.

Tip: S&CC, property tax and utilities are recurring costs of ownership that are easy to overlook when comparing a flat's instalment with rent.
