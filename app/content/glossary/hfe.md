The HFE letter brings three checks into one application: eligibility to buy an HDB flat, eligibility for CPF housing grants (and the amounts), and eligibility for an HDB housing loan, including the maximum loan. It is based on the household's details and income at the time of application.

For resale buyers who plan to take an HDB loan, a valid HFE letter is required before the seller can grant the Option to Purchase. The letter is valid for {policy:proposed.hfe.validity_months} months.

Worked example: e.g. a couple's letter states a maximum HDB loan of `S$450,000` and grants of `S$50,000`. Added to their CPF savings and cash, this gives a realistic ceiling for the flats they shortlist.

Tip: A new job, a change in income or a change in who is in the household can alter the outcome, so the letter reflects a point in time rather than a permanent entitlement.
