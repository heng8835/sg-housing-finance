Buying a flat involves legal work: checking the title, preparing the transfer, registering the mortgage and handling stamp duty. In an HDB resale, buyers may appoint HDB to act for them or engage a private law firm; buyers with a bank loan commonly use a firm that the bank accepts.

HDB's conveyancing fees follow published rules and scale with the price, with extra charges for items such as stamp, registration and survey fees. Each side pays its own legal costs. CPF Ordinary Account savings can generally be used for legal fees on the purchase.

Worked example: e.g. on a `S$600,000` flat, conveyancing and related fees typically come to a few thousand dollars — small relative to the price, but part of the money needed at completion.

Tip: HDB provides an online estimator for its conveyancing fees, which helps complete the upfront-cost picture before committing.
