At {policy:cpf.age.ra_formation}, savings from the Special and Ordinary Accounts move into the **Retirement Account** until the FRS is reached; savings above it stay available. The **Enhanced Retirement Sum** is {policy:cpf.retirement_sums.ers_multiple} times the FRS — the most you may put in for a higher payout.
