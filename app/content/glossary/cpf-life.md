CPF LIFE (Lifelong Income For the Elderly) uses the savings in your **Retirement Account (RA)** to pay you every month from age {policy:cpf.age.life_payout}, for life. The more you have in the RA when you turn {policy:cpf.age.ra_formation}, the larger the payout — so CPF used for a home earlier, which is not there to form the RA, means a smaller payout later.

The app's CPF LIFE figure is **approximate**: it scales CPF's published examples to your projected RA. The plan you choose, top-ups and starting the payout later change the amount and are not modelled. CPF's own planner gives your actual estimate.

Tip: Plan → CPF & retirement shows the estimate with and without the flat you picked, side by side.
