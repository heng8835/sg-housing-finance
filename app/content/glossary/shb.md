"Right-sizing" means moving to a smaller flat later in life. The Silver Housing Bonus rewards doing it in a way that adds to retirement income: you commit to a net increase in your **Retirement Account (RA)** of up to {policy:seniors.shb.full_topup} from the sale, and receive up to {policy:seniors.shb.bonus_max} for that part — plus up to {policy:seniors.shb.two_room_extra} more when the next home is a two-room Flexi flat.

At least one owner must be a Singapore Citizen aged {policy:seniors.shb.min_age} or older, household income within {policy:seniors.shb.income_ceiling} a month, and the smaller flat bought within {policy:seniors.shb.purchase_window_months} months of selling. CPF money refunded from the sale counts towards the top-up.

Tip: The **Options** card in Plan shows the right-sizing path with the flat you picked as the next home. CPF's page lists every condition.
