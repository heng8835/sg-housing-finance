ABSD is designed to moderate demand for additional homes and purchases by non-citizens. The rate is set by IRAS for each combination of residency status and number of residential properties owned. Singapore Citizens buying their first residential property pay none; Permanent Residents pay ABSD even on a first property, and rates rise steeply for second and later properties. Foreigners cannot buy HDB flats.

When joint buyers have different profiles, the highest applicable rate applies to the whole purchase. IRAS offers remission in some cases — for example, to certain married couples buying their first home together, or to owners who sell their previous flat within a set period.

Worked example: e.g. a couple who are both Permanent Residents buying a `S$500,000` resale flat as their first home would pay ABSD at the Permanent Resident first-property rate on `S$500,000`, on top of BSD. This app looks up that rate from the current IRAS table.

Tip: Any share in a residential property, including an inherited one, can count towards the number of properties owned, so checking the household's profile against IRAS's rules early avoids surprises.
