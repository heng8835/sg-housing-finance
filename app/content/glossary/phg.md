The PHG encourages families to live close together for mutual care and support. It applies to resale flats and is open to first-timers and second-timers alike, with no income ceiling, but each household can receive it only once.

There are two tiers: a larger amount to live together with parents or a married child in the same flat, and a smaller amount to live near them — within {policy:grant.phg.near_km} km. Singles have their own, smaller amounts.

Worked example: e.g. a couple comparing a flat within the distance limit of a parent's home with a similar flat further away would find the PHG narrows the price gap between the two.

Tip: The distance is measured between the two flats' addresses under HDB's rules, so a flat that looks close on a map may still fall outside the limit.
