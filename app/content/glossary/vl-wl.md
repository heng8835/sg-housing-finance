The Valuation Limit (VL) is the lower of the purchase price and the flat's value at the time of purchase. CPF Ordinary Account savings can be used for the downpayment and instalments until the total CPF used reaches the VL.

Beyond the VL, members can continue using OA savings only after setting aside the Basic Retirement Sum in CPF. For an HDB flat with an HDB loan, the reference point is the VL itself. With a bank loan, continued use is capped at the Withdrawal Limit (WL) of {policy:proposed.cpf.wl.multiple_of_vl} of the VL. Once a limit is reached and the conditions are not met, instalments must be paid in cash.

Worked example: e.g. a flat bought at `S$600,000` and valued at `S$590,000` has a VL of `S$590,000`. CPF used across all owners counts towards it, including the downpayment and every instalment paid from CPF.

Tip: These limits mainly bite late in a long loan, when years of CPF-funded instalments have added up.
