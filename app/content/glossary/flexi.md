Two-room Flexi flats are the smallest new HDB flats. Seniors aged {policy:seniors.flexi.min_age} or older can buy one on a **short lease**, chosen in steps, as long as it covers the youngest owner and spouse to at least age {policy:seniors.flexi.cover_to_age}. A shorter lease costs less, so more of the sale money from a larger flat can go to retirement. Household income must be within {policy:seniors.flexi.income_ceiling} a month.

Singles buying a new flat on their own are also offered two-room Flexi flats. Right-sizing to one can add to the **Silver Housing Bonus**.

Tip: The **Options** card in Plan lists the short-lease two-room Flexi as one of the paths, with whether you are eligible.
