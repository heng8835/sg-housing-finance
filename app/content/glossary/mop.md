The MOP keeps HDB flats for owner-occupation rather than short-term trading. Owners must physically live in the flat for the MOP — generally {policy:proposed.mop.years} years for resale flats — before they can sell it, rent out the whole flat or buy a private property.

The MOP is counted from the date the owners take possession of the flat. Periods when the whole flat is rented out do not count. Some newer flat types carry a longer MOP.

Worked example: e.g. a couple who complete a resale purchase this year can sell no earlier than {policy:proposed.mop.years} years after that date.

Tip: The MOP also matters when buying — a seller can only grant an Option to Purchase once they have met their own MOP.
