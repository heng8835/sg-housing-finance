Resale flats vary widely in condition. Some are ready to move into; others, especially older flats, may need rewiring, new plumbing, kitchen and bathroom works, or new flooring. These costs come on top of the purchase price.

Renovation is paid in cash or with a separate renovation loan; CPF savings and the housing loan cannot be used for it. HDB also sets renovation rules — some works need a permit — and contractors must be listed in HDB's Directory of Renovation Contractors.

Worked example: e.g. a household with `S$40,000` in cash that needs `S$25,000` for COV and fees has about `S$15,000` left for renovation. That may make a well-kept flat at a slightly higher price easier to fund than a cheaper flat needing major works.

Tip: When comparing flats, the likely renovation cost belongs in the comparison alongside the price difference.
