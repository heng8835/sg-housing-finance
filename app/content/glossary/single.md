HDB treats a buyer without a spouse or family nucleus as a single. The singles scheme is for **Singapore Citizens**: from age {policy:eligibility.single.min_age} a single can buy a resale flat of most types, or a new 2-room Flexi flat in any location. Singles may also buy together with other singles (the Joint Singles Scheme).

Grants and the HDB loan for singles use their own income ceiling of {policy:eligibility.income_ceiling.single} a month and smaller grant amounts than families.

A single **Permanent Resident** cannot buy an HDB flat; renting or a private home are the routes until citizenship.

Tip: In Your household, choose "Single" under Buying as — the flat types and grants then follow the singles rules.
