A flat bought from HDB, or a resale flat bought with a CPF housing grant, is a subsidised flat. If you sold one and then buy a second subsidised flat — a new flat from HDB or a new executive condo from a developer — you usually pay a resale levy for the second one.

The levy is a fixed amount set by the type of the first subsidised flat you sold. It is payable when you buy the second subsidised flat.

Tip: Ask HDB for the exact levy before you apply. The Plan tab shows it in the sale breakdown when you sell a subsidised flat and buy again.
