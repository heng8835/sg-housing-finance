In an HDB resale, the seller grants the buyer an Option to Purchase using HDB's standard form. To receive it, the buyer pays an option fee of up to {policy:proposed.resale.option_fee.max}. The buyer then has {policy:proposed.resale.otp.exercise_days} days to decide; exercising the option requires an exercise fee, with the two fees together capped at {policy:fees.option_exercise.max}.

Both fees are negotiable within those caps, form part of the resale price and must be paid in cash — CPF savings cannot be used for them. If the buyer does not exercise the option in time, the option fee goes to the seller.

Worked example: e.g. a buyer pays a `S$500` option fee. If they exercise the option, it counts towards the price; if they let it lapse, the `S$500` is lost.

Tip: Order matters — a buyer taking an HDB loan needs a valid HFE letter before the OTP is granted, and the Request for Value comes after it, so the valuation is not yet known when the option fee is paid.
