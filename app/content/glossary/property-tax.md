IRAS levies property tax every year on all properties, based on the Annual Value — the estimated yearly rent the property could fetch, excluding furniture. For an HDB flat that the owner lives in, concessionary owner-occupier rates apply: the lowest band of Annual Value is taxed at zero and higher bands at progressively higher rates.

Because HDB flats generally have modest Annual Values, many owner-occupiers pay little property tax, and the Government sometimes grants one-off rebates. If the whole flat is rented out, the owner-occupier rates stop applying and higher non-owner-occupier rates take over.

Worked example: e.g. a flat with an Annual Value of `S$20,000` is taxed at zero on the lowest band and at the owner-occupier rates only on the portion above it.

Tip: The owner-occupier rates apply to only one property — the one the owner actually lives in.
