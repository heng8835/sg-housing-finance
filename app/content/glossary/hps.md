HPS protects a household from losing its HDB flat if a borrower can no longer pay. If an insured member dies, is diagnosed with a terminal illness or becomes totally and permanently disabled, CPF settles their insured share of the outstanding loan directly with HDB or the bank.

It is required for members using CPF savings to pay the monthly instalments on an HDB flat. Cover lasts until the member reaches age {policy:proposed.hps.cover_to_age} or the loan is paid off, whichever comes first. Premiums are deducted yearly from the Ordinary Account and depend on the loan amount, tenure and age.

Worked example: e.g. a couple who pay `60%` and `40%` of the instalment would typically take shares of cover at least in those proportions, adding up to at least `100%` of the loan.

Tip: Because HPS is mortgage-reducing, the sum insured falls as the loan is repaid; it covers the home loan only, not other living costs.
