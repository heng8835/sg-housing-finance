Working Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents contribute part of their monthly wages to CPF, and their employers add more. A large share flows into the Ordinary Account, the main source of CPF money for buying a home.

OA savings can pay for the downpayment, stamp duty, legal fees and monthly instalments, within the limits set by CPF. The OA earns at least {policy:proposed.cpf.oa.rate} a year, and the HDB concessionary loan rate ({policy:rate.hdb.concessionary}) is pegged just above the OA rate.

Worked example: e.g. a couple with `S$80,000` combined in their OAs could put it towards the downpayment and fees, then pay part of the instalment from their ongoing monthly contributions.

Tip: Every dollar of OA used for housing stops earning CPF interest and must be returned with accrued interest when the flat is sold, so using CPF trades off against retirement savings.
