The EHG supports first-time buyers with lower and middle incomes. The amount depends on the household's average gross monthly income over the twelve months before applying: HDB's table sets out income bands, with the largest grant for the lowest band and smaller amounts as income rises, up to an income ceiling above which no EHG is given. Singles have their own, smaller table. At least one buyer must have been working continuously in that period.

The grant is credited to the buyers' CPF Ordinary Accounts and must go towards the flat, reducing the downpayment or loan. For resale flats it can be combined with the CPF Housing Grant and the Proximity Housing Grant. The full amount needs the remaining lease to cover the youngest buyer to age {policy:cpf.lease.cover_to_age}; otherwise it is pro-rated.

Worked example: e.g. a first-timer couple averaging `S$6,000` a month would find their tier in HDB's EHG table; a couple earning more would receive a smaller amount, or none.

Tip: The HFE letter states the exact EHG amount for the household.
