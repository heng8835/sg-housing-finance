# Changelog

User-facing changes. Rule values are not listed here — they live, dated and sourced, in
`app/policy/sg-policy.json` (the app's Learn sheet shows the rule-change log).

## Unreleased — v1.0.0

First public release of **SG Housing & Finance** (formerly the "HDB Resale Comparer").

### Explore (map)
- Every HDB block, including ones completed but never resold; colour by median price, $psf, number of sales,
  rent, commute time or "within my budget"; hexagons when zoomed out, number boxes when zoomed in.
- Floating block cards (up to three) with price trend, sales history and a "no resales yet" note for new blocks.
- Area prices for the view, a circle or a freehand shape; sales-history period slider.
- Layers: MRT and future lines, bus stops and services, schools, childcare (with vacancies), clinics and
  polyclinics, eldercare, parks, hawkers, malls, supermarkets, eateries, flood-prone points, former burial grounds.
- Saved map views; place search for blocks, streets, towns, MRT and schools.

### Compare and the flat brief
- Side-by-side shortlist: price against comparable recent sales ("Over-priced?", with the comparables), money rows
  from the same engine as Afford, remaining lease, commute to your places, family rows (P1 distance bands,
  childcare, clinics), environment rows and an eight-driver future-value scorecard (no single score).
- One-page printable flat brief, CSV download, and a share link that carries the flats only.

### Afford
- Most you can pay and what limits it, HDB vs bank loan, upfront cash vs CPF, grants, true monthly cost, rate
  stress test and repayment chart; counts the sale of your current flat when you plan one.
- Honest verdicts: "possible with a smaller loan", short-lease "up to" notes, eligibility checks before a verdict.
- Up to four named scenarios, compared side by side.

### Rent & Buy
- Which buying and renting paths are open to your household; is this rent fair; rent vs buy over time with an
  optional Monte-Carlo range; renting out your flat.

### Plan
- CPF with vs without the purchase per buyer, RA at 55 and CPF LIFE estimates (optional ranges), payout already
  received for buyers at the payout age.
- Sell then buy: sale proceeds, CPF refund and accrued interest estimate, step-by-step timeline and the gap
  between the two completions.
- Key dates with a calendar (.ics) download; options at 55+; primary schools by P1 distance band;
  BTO vs resale from a price and key-collection month you type, with the BTO payment stages.

### Learn and getting started
- "Start here": six quick questions on the first visit; tours of each tab.
- Glossary, four step-by-step guides with live numbers and short quizzes, the rule-change log.
- Three sample households to try the app without typing your own.

### Everywhere
- English and 中文; Simple mode with plain words and Pro mode with the detail; three text sizes.
- Installable, with an offline copy after one visit (map tiles still need a connection).
- Privacy by design: your household stays in this browser; "Forget my data" clears it; no analytics.

### Not in this version
- BTO project listings on the map (no licensed source yet) — BTO vs resale works from a price you type.
